§ 02 — The hard truth

The hard truth

Clients judge your site before they judge your work.

You could be the most talented designer, photographer, or writer in your city. But if your portfolio looks like it was thrown together in an afternoon, clients bounce — and you never know which one.

A weak site does not just fail to impress. It quietly costs you gigs. Especially when you are early in your career and every impression counts.

FAQ

Why $300?

Because talented freelancers deserve a site that looks serious without paying agency rates. I'm a student who builds fast with a tight stack — no agency overhead, so the price stays honest.

What if I'm not happy?

You get two revision rounds included. We iterate until it feels right. If something is genuinely off, we talk before it goes live.

Do you handle hosting?

Yes. I deploy on Vercel and hand over everything you need to own the site. Domain setup is available as an add-on.

What if I need more than 5 pages?

Extra pages are $40 each. Tell me what you need in the intake form and I will quote it upfront.

How fast can you really turn it around?

Most projects ship in about two weeks. First draft usually lands within a week of kickoff.

What do you need from me to get started?

A short intake form (below), links to your best work, and any brand assets you have. If you are starting from scratch, we can shape the direction together in the first call.

Do you work with my niche?

Photographers, designers, illustrators, motion artists, writers, stylists, and art directors — if you are a creative freelancer who needs to be taken seriously online, you are a fit.
